masks has two looks:

- The sign-in and account pages, and this site, use the rose window palette, drawn from the masks
  mark.
- `/manage` uses its own quieter palette, built for dense tables and forms.

Each one follows the reader's system setting for light or dark mode. This site also has a menu in
its header to choose one.

| Where | Set in |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in and account pages | `server/app/frontend/entrypoints/app.css` |
| `/manage` | `server/app/frontend/entrypoints/console.css` |
| This site | `docs/src/styles/global.css` |

## The mark

<img src={mark.src} class="mark" alt="The masks mark, a stained-glass rose window" />

The mark is a stained-glass rose window, traced. It was made from a photograph in four steps:

1. Smooth the photograph.
2. Snap it to thirteen glass colors, and treat anything near black as lead.
3. Trace each color as one vector layer.
4. Save the layers as one SVG file.

That file is `engine/public/masks-public/icon.svg`. It is the favicon everywhere, the brand in
`/manage`, and this site's logo.

The source image is 188 by 188 pixels, with 28 significant colors. Pure black is 49.3% of it. Every
value lands on `0x2a`, `0x55`, `0x7f`, `0xaa`, or `0xff`, so these colors are too strong to use as
they are. They set the hues that every other color comes from.

## The rose window palette

The palette has three families, named after the parts of a stained-glass window:

| Family | In a window | In masks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead** | The dark frame | The background and the grays. |
| **Came** | The brass and silver strips that join the panes | Rules, borders, focus rings, and small headings above a title. |
| **Glass** | The colored panes | Links, badges, status, notes, and chart marks. |

### Lead

In dark mode, this site's background is pure black, `#000000`, because black is half the window. In
light mode, it is a warm off-white, `#fcf9f7`. The grays above
it have a slight warm tint so they sit well next to brass.

Body text is `lead-800`, at a contrast of 16.8 to 1 on black. Dimmed text in tables and captions is
`lead-600`, at 8.7 to 1.

The sign-in and account pages use a dark warm brown, `#15110e`, in dark mode, and white in light
mode. The mark shines across the top of the page as blurred light. Panels are frosted, with a thin
rim, and let that light through.

### Came

Came marks structure. A brass border shows where one part ends and the next begins. It never means
"this needs attention".

### Glass

Glass marks meaning: a link, a badge, a status, or a note. There are ten hues, and four of them sit
between red and yellow, so the palette feels warm.

Each hue has two shades:

- a light shade for a dark background
- a dark shade for a white background

Each contrast ratio is measured against the background that shade is used on.

| Hue | Angle | On black | Ratio | On white | Ratio | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ruby | 27° | `#ff867a` | 8.94 | `#b53530` | 5.97 | danger |
| ember | 47° | `#ff8f53` | 9.31 | `#b04000` | 5.87 | primary action |
| amber | 68° | `#f19c29` | 9.53 | `#a44f00` | 5.68 | accent |
| gold | 92° | `#d4ad03` | 9.78 | `#8b6200` | 5.46 | warning |
| verdant | 140° | `#78c966` | 10.35 | `#267e05` | 5.16 | accent |
| emerald | 165° | `#1dcf98` | 10.43 | `#008450` | 4.76 | success |
| cyan | 195° | `#00cecf` | 10.73 | `#008386` | 4.57 | accent |
| sky | 228° | `#00c2fe` | 10.15 | `#0077b2` | 4.90 | info |

| violet | 292° | `#b49dff` | 9.21 | `#6e50be` | 5.91 | accent |

Ember is the primary button. Ruby is the button for an action that destroys something.

## /manage

`/manage` uses a neutral gray palette with one warm accent, a vermilion orange, for the primary
action, links, focus rings, and charts. Its corners are rounder than on the sign-in pages, and its
text is smaller, so tables and forms fit more on a screen.

| | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#f4f4f5` | `#0a0a0b` |
| Panels | `#ffffff` | `#131315` |
| Text | `#18181b` | `#ededef` |
| Accent | `#c2410c` | `#ff7a4a` |
| Danger | `#b0183f` | `#ff7396` |

## Type

The sign-in pages and `/manage` use the system font stack, so text appears in each device's own
typeface. Code, client IDs, URLs, scopes, and figures use the system monospace font.

This site uses Helvetica Neue, with Helvetica and Arial as fallbacks.

There are no display fonts and no web fonts. Spacing, grid, color, and hierarchy do the work.
