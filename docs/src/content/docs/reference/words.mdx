`server/config/locales/en/` · `server/app/frontend/manage/`

Every screen says the same thing the same way. Two audiences read masks, and a few words change
between them: **managers** in `/manage`, and **people** on the sign-in pages, their account page
and the mail masks sends.

`actor` stays in code, in the GraphQL schema and in these concept pages, where it names the model.
It never reaches a screen.

## Verbs and nouns

**Sign in**, **sign out** and **sign up** are verbs; **sign-in**, **sign-out** and **signup** are
nouns. A button says *Sign in*; a chart counts *sign-ins*; a policy decides whether *signup* is open.

## Events

An event's label is written once, in `events.yml`, and served to the console as `label` on `Event`
and `EventAction`. The account page reads the same file. Labels are a noun and a past participle —
*Passkey added*, *Client archived*, *Device sign-in refused* — and a change is always *changed*,
never *edited* or *updated*.

## Buttons and empty lists

A page's add button names what it adds: *Add person*, *Add policy*, *Add provider*, *Add adapter*.
The form's own submit is *Add* or *Save*.

A list with nothing in it says *Nothing yet.*, or *None.* where nothing will ever arrive on its own.
A link to more of a list says *All activity*.
