---
title: The manage console
description: Where everything lives in /manage, and what each page and tab holds.
---

`/manage` is where a tenant is run: its actors, clients, organizations, providers, and settings. It
signs in through masks like any other app, so only an actor with a `masks:manage` scope reaches it.
Everything it does goes through the [manage API](/reference/manage/), which a script can call too.

## Who can do what

`masks:manage` can do everything. The narrower [manage roles](/guides/security/#manage-roles) see the
same pages, and the console says at the top what they cannot change:

| Role | In the console |
| --- | --- |
| `masks:manage:read` | Reads everything and changes nothing. |
| `masks:manage:support` | Helps actors. Settings, keys, and other managers need an owner. |
| `masks:manage:security` | Changes keys, clients, providers, and policies. Actors need support or an owner. |

## Finding your way

The bar along the top holds **Overview**, **Actors**, **Organizations**, and **Clients**. Settings
open from the same bar and have their own tabs. On a phone, the bar moves to the bottom of the screen.

Every page and tab has its own address, so a link to `/manage/clients/abc/access` opens that client's
**Access** tab, and a filtered list such as `/manage/actors?lens=waiting` keeps its filter. Opening
one of these links while signed out signs you in and comes back to it.

## Overview

The overview opens with whatever needs a manager:

- actors waiting for approval, linked to that filter on **Actors**
- actors who [asked for help](/guides/features/#second-factors) signing in
- events from the last day worth a look, such as a refused sign-in, a reused refresh token, or a
  blocked device, with the latest named, linked to **Activity**

A new tenant also sees **Getting started**: add a mail service, register a first client, and invite
someone. Each step goes away once it is done, and **Hide** puts one away in this browser.

Below that are counts of actors, clients, organizations, and devices, a chart of recent sign-ins, the
first few organizations with their members, and the latest activity.

## Actors

The list marks each actor who is a manager, has a second factor, is invited, has an unconfirmed
address, is waiting for approval, is asking for help, is suspended, or has a blocked device. Its filters are **Everyone**, **Invited**,
**Awaiting approval**, **Asking for help**, **Managers**, and **Suspended**. **Invite many actors**
takes a list of addresses at once.

**Devices** sits under the same page, filtered by **Allowed**, **Blocked**, and **Never signed in**.
See [sessions and devices](/guides/features/#sessions-and-devices).

An actor's page names anything waiting on a manager at the top, such as an approval or a request for
help, with the button that settles it. Its tabs:

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Their nickname, name, and email address, more profile claims, their photo, and **Suspend or delete**. |
| **Access** | Their scopes, their [groups](/guides/features/#groups), the IDs a directory provisioned them with, the apps they have let in, and their connected accounts. |
| **Sign-in** | Their password, second factors, and passkeys, where they are signed in, and their tokens. |
| **Activity** | Everything they did, and everything done to them. |

## Organizations

The list shows each organization with its members, owners, and open invitations, and marks one with
no owner. An organization's tabs are **Members**, **Sign-in**, **Roles**, **Activity**, and
**General**. [Organizations in /manage](/guides/organizations/#organizations-in-manage) says what each
holds.

## Clients

The list marks a client that registered itself, whether or not a manager has approved it, and an
archived one. **Add client** registers one by hand, or from
[a known product](/guides/features/#adding-a-known-app). A client's tabs:

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Its name and how it authenticates, its SAML settings for a SAML application, what actors are shown about it, such as its home page, how long its tokens last, and archiving it. |
| **Access** | [Who may sign in](/guides/features/#who-may-sign-in-to-an-app), and the scopes it may ask for. |
| **Endpoints** | Its redirect and logout URIs, its keys, and its [authorization details](/guides/security/#rich-authorization-requests). |
| **Usage** | Who has let it in, its live tokens, its activity, and the namespaces it claimed. |

## Settings

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | The tenant's name and branding, how long activity is kept, dynamic registration, signing keys, [groups](/guides/features/#groups), domains, and [namespaces](/guides/core-concepts/#namespaces). |
| **Policies** | The [sign-in policies](/guides/features/#sign-in-policies), and which is the default. |
| **Providers** | The identity providers and MCP servers actors sign in or connect with. |
| **Directory** | The [LDAP](/guides/ldap/) server, and [provisioning](/guides/enterprise/) tokens. |
| **Messages** | The services that send mail and texts, under **Sending**, and the wording of each message, under **Templates**. |
| **Activity** | The tenant's whole log, filtered by kind of event and exported, and [event streams](/guides/event-streams/) under **Streams**. |

The tab bar also names who is signed in, and holds **Sign out** and **Forget client**, which drops
this browser's manage client and asks you to approve a new one. The old client stays under
**Clients** until it is archived.

### A provider

The providers list shows each provider with its protocol and marks for what it does: adding the
actors it signs in, trusting the addresses it confirms, accepting its
[security events](/guides/shared-signals/), and lending clients an actor's account. It also marks a
provider with no client secret. **Add provider** starts from a preset such as Google or Microsoft,
or from a blank OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.0, SAML 2.0, or MCP provider. A provider's tabs:

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Setup** | Its name, the values to give the identity provider, its endpoints, its credentials, and archiving it. |
| **Sign-in** | The email domains it signs in, the scopes a new actor holds, whether its addresses are trusted, its security events, and where each claim comes from. |
| **Clients** | [Delegation](/guides/connecting-via-sdk/#set-up-the-provider): whether clients may use an actor's account there, and what they may do. |
| **Connected accounts** | Every actor who has connected an account through it. |

An MCP server has no **Sign-in** tab, and a SAML provider has no **Clients** tab. Changes wait in a
bar at the bottom of the page until **Save** or **Discard**.

## Activity

Each event reads as a sentence, such as _Signed in_ or _Token refused_, with who did it, which client,
and when. On the overview and on an actor's or organization's page, the same event repeated in a row folds into one
line with a count. Hovering an event's details shows them in full. The [words](/reference/words/) page names each kind of event.
