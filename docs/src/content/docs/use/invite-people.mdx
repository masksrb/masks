---
title: Invite and approve people
description: Let masks send email, invite people one at a time or many at once, let people sign up, and approve them.
---

Managers invite people from **Manage this server**. Open it from the top of your account page.

## Let masks send email first

Invitations, password resets, and sign-in codes go out by email. Until masks can send email, a
manager passes each invitation link along by hand.

1. Open **Manage this server**. The overview shows **Getting started** with the step
   **Let masks send email**. Choose **Add a mail service**.
2. Under **Mail**, choose **Add a service**, pick the **Service**, and enter what it asks for,
   such as the server and a password.
3. Choose **Add**.
4. Enter your own address under **Send a test to** and choose **Send a test**. Check your inbox.

The same page is under **Settings**, then **Messages**, then **Sending**.

## Invite one person

1. Open **Actors** and choose **Add actor**.
2. Enter a **Nickname**, an **Email**, or both.
3. Leave **Password** blank and choose **Send the invitation**.

masks emails the person. The email is headed **You have been invited to** your server's name, and
its button says **Choose a password**. The link works once, and nothing happens until they open
it. It stays valid for 7 days unless a manager changes that under **Settings**, then **General**,
then **Links**.

When masks cannot send email yet, the screen shows the link instead, with **Pass this link along
yourself. It works once.** Send it to the person however you like.

To make the account with a password you choose, enter one under **Password** and choose
**Add actor**. Tell the person the password yourself. They can sign in right away.

## Invite many people

1. Open **Actors** and choose **Invite many**.
2. Paste the addresses under **Email addresses**, one per line, or separated by spaces or
   commas.
3. To put everyone in a group at once, tick the groups under **Put them in**.
4. Choose **Send invitations**.

masks lists each address with what happened to it: **invited**, **is already an actor**, or
**is not an address masks can invite**.

## See who has not accepted

In **Actors**, choose the **Invited** filter. Each person is marked **Invited, has not signed in
yet**. Open the person and choose **Resend invitation** to send a fresh link. The earlier link
stops working.

## Let people sign up on their own

By default, only invited people get an account. To let anyone sign up:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Policies**, and open the default policy.
2. Under **Signup**, turn on **Open**.
3. Under **Email domains**, list the domains that may sign up, such as `example.com`. Leave it
   empty to allow any address.
4. Under **Confirmation**, choose what a new account has to do before it can sign in:

   |                      |                                                                 |
   | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | **None**             | Nothing. The account works at once.                             |
   | **Emailed code**     | Enter a six-digit code masks emails them.                       |
   | **Emailed link**     | Open a link masks emails them.                                  |
   | **Manager approval** | Wait for a manager to approve them.                             |

5. Save the policy.

With **Open** on, the sign-in page says **New here? Enter your email and masks makes you an
account.**

## Approve a new account

With **Manager approval** on, a new account sees **Waiting for a manager** after it signs up, and
cannot sign in yet. Every manager gets an email headed with the person's nickname and
**is waiting to be let in to** your server's name, with a **Review the account** button.

1. Open **Actors**. The **Actors** button at the top shows how many people are waiting.
2. Choose the **Awaiting approval** filter.
3. Open the person. The top of their page says **Awaiting approval**.
4. Choose **Approve**.

masks emails them **Your account is ready**, and they can sign in.

## Suspend, restore, or delete someone

Open the person in **Actors**. On their **Profile** tab, under **Suspend or delete**:

- **Suspend** signs them out everywhere and refuses every sign-in until a manager chooses
  **Restore**. Everything they had is kept.
- **Delete** removes them for good, along with their passkeys, sessions, and photo.

Nobody can suspend or delete the last manager.
