---
title: Your account page
description: Where each person changes how they sign in, sees their devices, and checks what has been happening to their account.
---

Open your masks server in a browser and sign in. Your account page opens. It is where you change
how you sign in, see the devices you are signed in on, and check what has been happening to your
account.

Changes that matter, such as a new password or a new passkey, ask you to sign in again when your
last sign-in was more than 15 minutes ago.

## Your apps

The top of the page lists the apps you may sign in to, under **Your apps**. Choose one to open it.
masks signs you in on the way.

A manager also sees **Manage this server** at the top. **Sign out** signs you out of masks in this
browser.

## Signing in

|                       |                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Email address**     | Password resets and confirmations go here. To change it, enter a **New email address**, choose **Send a code**, enter the **Code** masks sends, and choose **Confirm**. |
| **Phone number**      | SMS codes go here. It changes the same way, with a code sent by text message. **Remove** takes it off the account.                                                  |
| **Password**          | **Change password** asks for your current password and the new one. Changing it signs you out everywhere else. An account with no password can **Set a password**. |
| **Passkeys**          | **Set this up** adds a passkey. Each one shows when it was last used, and **Remove** takes it off.                                                                   |
| **Authenticator app** | Scan the code with the app, enter **The code the app shows**, and choose **Turn on**. **Remove** stops asking for its codes.                                        |
| **Backup codes**      | How many you have left. **Make new codes** replaces them.                                                                                                            |
| **Email codes**       | **Turn on** to confirm each sign-in with a code sent to your confirmed email address.                                                                               |
| **SMS codes**         | **Turn on** to confirm each sign-in with a code sent to your confirmed phone number.                                                                                |
| **Approve a sign-in** | On a device you trust, enter the **Code on the other screen** to approve a sign-in happening somewhere else.                                                        |

See [signing in](/use/signing-in/) for how each of these works.

## Devices

**Devices** lists every phone, tablet, and computer that has signed in to your account, with when
it was last seen and where from. The one you are on is marked **This device**.

- **Sign this device out** ends every sign-in on that device. It also stops that device from
  skipping the second step.
- A device that skips the second step says **This device does not have to enter a code.**
- Give a device a name and choose **Save name**.

## Organizations

**Organizations** lists each organization you belong to and your role there. An invitation waits
under **Waiting for you** until you choose **Join** or **Decline**. An owner of an organization
adds and removes its members here too. See [organizations](/use/organizations/).

## Apps and accounts

|                        |                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Apps you have let in** | Each app you have signed in to, with what you agreed it may see. **Revoke access** makes it ask again the next time you sign in.                                                 |
| **Connected accounts** | An account somewhere else, such as a work account, that is connected to this one. **Connect** adds one, and **Disconnect** removes it. An app that can use a connected account while you are away is listed, with **Stop**. |
| **App passwords**      | Some apps, such as a media server on a television, cannot do the second step. An app password lets one of them sign in as you. See [app passwords](#app-passwords).              |

### App passwords

The section appears once an app you may sign in to accepts app passwords. A manager turns that on
for the app.

1. Enter a **Name**, such as **Jellyfin on the TV**, and pick the **App**.
2. Choose **Make app password**.
3. Copy the password into the app. masks shows it once.

An app password works for that app and nothing else. Your own password and passkeys stay as they
are. **Revoke** ends one. An account holds up to 20.

## Details

Under **Name**, change **Your name** and your **Nickname**, then choose **Save**. Other people
see the nickname, and you can sign in with it. **Change your photo** uploads a picture of you.

## Emails we send you

masks emails you when something changes on your account, such as a new passkey or a sign-in from a
new device. **Emails we send you** lists each kind. Turn off the ones you do not want and choose
**Save**. A changed or reset password and an unusual sign-in are always announced.

These emails go only to a confirmed email address.

## Download your account

**Download** saves a file with your profile, how you sign in, your organizations, apps, connected
accounts, devices, and the activity masks has kept. It holds no passwords, codes, or keys.

## Delete your account

At the bottom of the page, type your nickname, or your email address when you have no nickname,
and choose **Delete my account**. Deleting signs you out of every app and device and removes your
passkeys, connected accounts, and photo. It cannot be undone. The last manager cannot delete their
own account.

## Activity

**Activity** lists what has happened to your account lately: sign-ins, changes, and apps let in.
Anything you do not recognize is worth changing your password over.
