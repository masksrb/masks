---
title: Organizations
description: Customers inside your server. Each one has its own members, and each member holds a role there.
---

An organization is a customer inside your server, such as a company you serve. It has members,
and each member holds one role there. Every organization offers the roles **owner** and
**member**, and it can offer more, such as **billing**. A person can belong to several
organizations, or to none.

An app that cares about organizations learns which one a person signed in for, and their role.
A person who belongs to several is asked **Choose an organization** when they sign in to such an
app.

| Who       | Where                                           | Can                                                                              |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A manager | **Manage this server**, under **Organizations** | Add and archive organizations. Set how members sign in. Add, promote, and remove anyone. |
| An owner  | Their own account page, under **Organizations** | Invite, promote, and remove the members of their own organization.               |
| A member  | Their own account page                          | See their role. Accept or decline an invitation. Leave.                          |

## Add an organization

1. Open **Manage this server**, then **Organizations**, and choose **Add organization**.
2. Enter a **Name**. masks fills in the **Key** from the name. Apps ask for the organization by
   its key, and it cannot change later.
3. Choose **Add organization** again. You become its first owner.
4. Add the customer's own owner as a member, with the **owner** role.
5. Once they accept, you can remove yourself.

## Invite members

Adding someone to an organization invites them. Nothing changes for them until they accept.

From **Manage this server**:

1. Open the organization and choose **Add member**.
2. Enter the person's email address and choose a role.
3. Choose **Invite**.

From an owner's own account page, under **Organizations**:

1. Enter the address under **Add someone by email**.
2. Choose a **Role**.
3. Choose **Add**.

masks emails the person. The email is headed **Join** and the organization's name, and names the
role and who invited them. Someone who has no account yet gets an account invitation first. Once
they have an account, the organization waits on their account page.

An owner can invite an address that the server's sign-up rules admit, or one at an email domain
the organization has proven. An owner sends at most 50 invitations a day.

## Accept an invitation

The invitation waits on the person's account page, under **Waiting for you**. It shows who sent
it, the role, when it expires, and the address it went to. The person chooses **Join** or
**Decline**.

- Only an account with that email address, confirmed, can accept it. An account whose address is
  not confirmed is asked to confirm it under **Email address** first.
- An invitation sent to another address says where it went. The person signs in with that address,
  or asks an owner to invite the address on this account.
- Declining removes the invitation. An owner has to invite the person again.

## Send an invitation again

An invitation expires two weeks after it was last sent, unless a manager changes that under
**Settings**, then **General**, then **Links**.

1. Open the organization's members, in **Manage this server** or on an owner's account page.
2. Find the person. An open invitation is marked **invited**. An expired one is marked
   **expired**.
3. Choose **Send again**.

Sending again emails the person and starts the two weeks over. It can be done once an hour.

## Roles

1. Open the organization in **Manage this server** and open its **Roles** tab.
2. Enter the role under **Offer another role** and choose **Add**.

A role uses lowercase letters, digits, dashes, and underscores, up to 40 characters. A role cannot
be removed while someone holds it.

To change someone's role, find them in the members list and choose **Set role**.

## Remove members and leave

- An owner or a manager chooses **Remove** next to a member. Anything the person signed in to as
  a member stops working.
- A member chooses **Leave** on their own account page. An owner has to invite them again.
- **Withdraw** takes back an invitation nobody has accepted yet.

An organization always keeps an owner. The only owner cannot leave, step down, or be removed until
someone else is an owner.

## What members are told

masks emails a member when someone else changes their role or removes them, and when the
organization is archived while they are signed in to an app for it. A member who makes the change
themselves, such as by leaving, gets no email. Each person can turn these emails off on their
account page, under **Emails we send you**.

## Archive an organization

Open the organization in **Manage this server**, open its **General** tab, and archive it. Nobody
can sign in as a member of it until a manager chooses **Restore**. Members keep their roles, so a
restore puts everything back.

## For developers

An organization can have its own sign-in rules, its own provider, and its own directory. What
apps read about organizations, and how they ask for one, is in
[organizations for developers](/build/organizations/).
