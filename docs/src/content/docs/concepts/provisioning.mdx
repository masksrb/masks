---
title: Provisioning
description: An identity provider adds, changes, suspends and removes people over SCIM 2.0.
---

An organisation that already keeps its people in Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace or JumpCloud does
not want to keep a second list in masks. SCIM 2.0 ([RFC 7643](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7643),
[RFC 7644](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7644)) lets that provider do it: when somebody joins
they are added here, when they are renamed they are renamed here, and when they leave they are
switched off here in the same minute.

Each tenant answers at its own base URL:

```
https://demo.masks.example/scim/v2
```

## Connecting a provider

In the console, under **Settings → Provisioning**, issue a token, give it a label the provider will
recognise, and choose how long it lives — a year at most. It is shown once. Paste the base URL and
the token into the provider as a bearer token. The list shows when each token was last used, and
revoking one refuses whatever provisions with it from then on.

A client can provision instead of a token: an approved client allowed `masks:scim` asks for its own
token with [`client_credentials`](/concepts/clients/), addressed to the base URL as its `resource`.

## What is provisioned

`/Users` is read, filtered, created, replaced with `PUT`, changed with `PATCH` and deleted.

| SCIM | masks |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | the actor's `uuid` |
| `userName` | the nickname, or the email when it is an address |
| `externalId` | kept, unique in the tenant, and filterable |
| `name`, `displayName` | the name claims |
| `emails` | the primary email, taken as confirmed |
| `phoneNumbers`, `photos`, `profileUrl`, `locale`, `timezone` | the matching claims |
| `active` | whether the person is suspended |
| `password` | set, if a provider sends one |

A provisioned person holds the standard scopes and has no password unless one was sent. They sign
in through a [provider](/concepts/providers/) that trusts the same email, or by accepting an
invitation a manager sends. Filters understand `eq`, `ne`, `co`, `sw`, `ew`, `pr` and the
comparisons, joined by `and`, on `userName`, `externalId`, `emails.value`, `displayName`,
`name.givenName`, `name.familyName`, `active` and the `meta` dates. Every response carries a weak
`ETag`, and a stale `If-Match` is refused.

`/ServiceProviderConfig`, `/ResourceTypes` and `/Schemas` describe the rest. Groups, bulk
operations and sorting are not offered.

## Suspending

`active: false` suspends somebody, and so does **Suspend** on their page in the console. They are
signed out of every session, every token they hold stops working at once — refresh tokens included
— and signing in is refused after the first factor, so a stranger typing their name learns nothing.
`active: true` restores them. `DELETE` removes them outright, the way deleting in the console does.

## What a provider cannot do

A provisioning token is worth a great deal, so it is worth less than a manager:

- it never sets the password or email of anybody holding a `masks:` scope, because either would be
  the first step to taking over their account
- it cannot suspend or delete the last person who manages masks here
- it cannot change anybody's scopes

Every change is written down as the person's activity, marked as having come through SCIM.
