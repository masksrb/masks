---
title: Actors
description: An account on a tenant — who signs in, and what they may ask for.
---

An actor is an account. It carries a nickname, optionally an email, and a list of scopes that bounds
everything it can be granted.

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| `nickname` | the identifier typed at sign-in; renameable without a new account |
| `email` / `email_verified_at` | changing the address takes its verification with it |
| `scopes` | the ceiling on what any token for this actor may carry |
| `activated_at` | unset while an invitation is outstanding |

Second factors are a TOTP authenticator, passkeys, and backup codes. A passkey can serve as both
factors at once.

Three predicates decide what an actor may have:

- `holds?(scope)` — is this exact scope in their list
- `withheld(scopes)` — which of these they cannot be granted
- `grant!(scopes)` — add scopes, as claiming a [namespace](/concepts/namespaces/) does

The first actor on a tenant is made by the first-run screen and holds `masks:manage`. After that,
accounts are invited or created from the console.
